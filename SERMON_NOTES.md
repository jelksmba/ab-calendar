# Sermon notes

The weekly sermon update reads this file before changing the "Latest sermon" card.
Add one line for a Sunday when service is cancelled or you want a special message.
Dates are the Sunday's date.

Formats:
- `YYYY-MM-DD | keep` leaves the prior sermon on the card.
- `YYYY-MM-DD | message | Your message here` shows your message on the card instead.
