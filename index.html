      Threat Levels

      LevelWhat you seeResponse
      1Curious, but calmObserve, engage gently, offer help, and build rapport.
      2Rule BreakerAddress behavior respectfully, set limits, stay calm, and de-escalate if pushback increases.
      3Verbally DisruptiveEngage with clear, calm verbal de-escalation. Ask to step aside and talk while monitoring body language.
      4Threatening or AggressiveMaintain distance. Keep low and calm tone. Signal/call for backup. Try to defuse, but be ready to disengage or call LEO.
      5Actively DangerousDisengage if possible. Secure the safety of others. Call LEO and engage physically only if necessary, trained, and justified.
      6Active AssailantUse active shooter protocol, move to cover or concealment. Notify LEO and stop the threat if capable and trained to do so.

      SOPs

      The key steps from each SOP in the AB Playbook. Tap one to open it. After any incident: file an incident report and debrief with the team.

      Watch & Locate

      BOLO ProtocolMatch the threat level, then act. AB traffic on Channel 9.
      1
      Low. Not considered dangerous; information only.
      Observe and report. Don't engage without backup or verification.
      2
      Moderate. Possible non-violent crime, no known violent history. May flee or resist.
      Approach with caution; detain with backup if necessary.
      3
      High. Potentially armed, dangerous, or violent history.
      Do not approach alone. Treat as high-risk.
      4
      Critical. Immediate, extreme danger (active shooter, violent person).
      Broadcast to all units immediately. Initiate lockdown or alerts.
      Missing ChildAlert, lock the exits, search by zone. Not found in 10 minutes: call 911.
      Not found within 10 minutes → call 911.

      1 · Respond immediately

      • Alert the nearest staff member and the Child Safety Coordinator.
      • Secure the area. No one leaves the premises until further notice.
      • Get the details: full name, age, description (height, weight, hair, clothing), last place and time seen, special needs. Get the parent's contact info.

      2 · Search

      • Split into search teams by zone: classrooms, restrooms, playgrounds, parking, common areas.
      • Search every area systematically. Stay in touch by radio.
      • Put someone on every exit with the child's description.

      3 · Communicate

      • Keep the parent informed of every step.
      • Be discreet with the congregation. Keep people calm.

      4 · After

      • Confirm the child's identity, reunite with the parent, and do a quick wellness check.
      • Incident report and debrief.

      People & Presence

      ABC Disruptive GuestServant Leader spots it, AB defuses it, LEO steps in if needed.

      A · Servant Leader

      • First to see or engage the disruptive guest.
      • Alerts the ABs for help.

      B · Armor Bearer

      • Defuse it discreetly, without confrontation.
      • If there's hostility, at least two ABs are present and the rest stay on alert.
      • If it escalates, call the LEO on duty.

      C · Law Enforcement

      • Steps in to de-escalate.
      • May escort the person off the premises.
      • After review, a no-trespass order may be issued.
      Spiritual ManifestationStay calm, notify the prayer team, approach with two. Dignity first.

      Signs

      • Sudden emotional outbursts.
      • Screaming, hissing, or mocking.
      • Collapse without a medical cause.
      • Disruption during altar call or worship.

      Response

      • Stay calm. Don't escalate, provoke, or label anyone.
      • Discreetly notify the prayer team (SRL) or a designated pastor or intercessor.
      • If it's unsafe or disruptive, approach with two team members: “We're here to help. Let's step out and pray with you.”
      • Escort them to a prayer area or side room.
      No restraint unless there's an immediate threat. No deliverance unless trained and authorized.

      After

      • Debrief with the team and pastors.
      • Incident report: time, place, what happened, who was involved, actions taken, who was notified.
      • Pray together afterward.

      Life Safety

      Fire EmergencyPull the alarm, call 911, clear the building, headcount. No re-entry.

      1 · Respond immediately

      • Activate the fire alarm.
      • Notify the Fire Safety Coordinator.
      • Call 911 and give the church address: 3350 Greenbriar Pkwy SW.
      • Use an extinguisher only if the fire is small and you're trained.

      2 · Evacuate

      • Announce the evacuation clearly and calmly.
      • Use the marked routes to the assembly area. No elevators.
      • Check every room, including restrooms and storage.
      • Help anyone with mobility challenges.
      • Do a headcount at the assembly area.

      3 · Communicate

      • Tell the fire department who may still be inside and where.
      • Keep leadership and the congregation updated.
      No one goes back in until the fire department clears it.
      Medical EmergencyScene safe, call for help, care if trained. Don't move them.

      1 · Respond immediately

      • Figure out what's happening and make sure the scene is safe.
      • Alert the nearest staff member, medical volunteer, or First Aid Coordinator.
      • Call 911 if it's life-threatening or beyond what we can handle.
      • First aid, CPR, or AED if you're trained and it's safe.
      Don't move the person unless they're in immediate danger.

      2 · Communicate

      • Contact their emergency contact if known.
      • Keep leadership informed. Be discreet with the congregation.

      3 · When EMS arrives

      • Guide responders to the person.
      • Share any medical history you know.
      • Clear space around them so responders can work.
      Active ShooterAct immediately. Whoever is closest has the right of way.
      All steps happen immediately. Whoever is closest to the threat has the right of way.
      • Firearm at low-ready. Only fire with a clear shot and no people in the way.
      • Command people near you to get down and take cover. Be loud and firm.
      • If the threat is outside the room, lock down and keep people in place.
      • AB1 and AB2 cover the pastor and first family, move them to the worship team staging area, and lock down.
      • Roamers move to the threat. Only give AB1 and AB2 the call to move (extraction or greenroom) once you can see it's handled.
      • Listen to your radio for direction.
      Know your post's assignment. The full position-by-position steps are in the AB Playbook.
